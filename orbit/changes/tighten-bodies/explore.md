# Exploration: tighten-bodies

## Premise

The third stage of `reduce-orbit-phase1` (its charter; the brief's section 4.3), with RG2's structural fixes. Four
tightenings: the decision-writing bullet restructured into labelled sub-bullets with its content unchanged (R-rop-7);
Guardrails sections that restate the steps removed, keeping any line that says something the steps do not; provenance
(stage tags, ruling ids, checked-mirror notes, owning-spec citations in headings) removed from the bodies; and the
walk's description shortened. And RG2: reference files one level deep, a table of contents on every reference file over
100 lines, descriptions that are valid YAML within 1,024 characters. Exit: RG1 (bodies at most 350,000 characters,
apply at most 17,500) and RG2 met, no wording an agent acts on removed (shown by tests), ceilings lowered, RG0 holds.

Measured at `a5ba0f0c` (`npm run size:report`, and a project generated by `orbit init --tools claude`), 2026-11-17:

- Bodies total 369,392 characters; RG1 needs 19,392 off. Apply is 18,887; RG1 needs 1,387 off it.
- The decision-writing bullet is 2,860 characters, byte-equal in 14 bodies (40,040 in all).
- Guardrails blocks are in 9 bodies (apply, archive, bulk-archive, continue, ff, new, propose, sync-specs as
  `**Guardrails**`; explore as `## Guardrails`, 985 characters). All are upstream text. Apply's carry three
  `(chain: decision)` tags.
- Backticked spec-capability citations in bodies: 120 across 15 bodies, about 6,300 characters with their
  `per`/`see` framing (rough, by regex); headings citing a spec: 17 in 8 bodies; `Mirror of` / checked-mirror notes:
  19 in 7 bodies (explore 5, propose 5).
- RG2 today: three descriptions are not valid YAML (address-reviews, architect, review: an unquoted `: ` inside the
  value); address-reviews' is 1,140 characters, over 1,024. Eleven distinct reference files run over 100 lines, none
  with a table of contents (`decision-records.md`, shipped in 14 skills, is one at 111). Review's
  `external-handoff.md` names `prompt-template.md` and `mode-sections.md`, which the body does not (the two-level
  chain `skill_body_convention.md` names); its `passes-<mode>.md` files are named by pattern.

What this exploration has to settle: whether the four tightenings reach RG1 (the architecture doc projects about 4,400
over), what each removes and how "acted on" is shown by test, which requirements and tests move, and how each RG2 fix
is cut.
