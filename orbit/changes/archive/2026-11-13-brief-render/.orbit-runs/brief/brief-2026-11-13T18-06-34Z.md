# Brief: change brief-render

This brief says what this change did, from what its history holds. Text under a heading is quoted from the change's records as they were written; orbit writes the rest.

- Change `brief-render`: active, at orbit/changes/brief-render/
- Program: architecture doc `automate-dev-flow`, stage `program-brief`

## What was built

A new command, orbit brief, prints the brief of one change or of a whole program in plain language: what was built, the requirements added or changed, the new terms and seams, every ledger entry, every stop with its answer or marked open, a line saying tripwires are not installed, the deviations from the charter, and the records no reader has checked. It writes nothing unless asked to save, and prints nothing at all when any part cannot be read, naming what it could not read. It reads every way past archives have recorded whether their requirements were synced, so finished programs get a brief too. Asked to save, it keeps a change's brief in the change's own history, never overwriting an earlier copy, and prints a fingerprint of the saved text that a later sign-off can cite. Every archive now saves the change's brief before the sync, so the copy moves with the change.

## New terms

- **fingerprint**: a short code computed from a file's exact contents, which changes if any character of the file changes (defined in ledger-2026-11-13T15-27-47Z.json, account-2026-11-13T17-45-17Z.json)
- **sync**: the archive's step that copies a change's requirement changes into the project's specs (defined in account-2026-11-13T17-45-17Z.json)
- **the reading step's brief**: what orbit gives the separate reader who checks the wording of the decision records during a review (defined in ledger-2026-11-13T18-03-53Z.json)

## New seams

None. The archive saves the brief without asking or showing anything, and a brief it cannot save is noted only in the archive's own record; the brief command runs only when someone types it.
