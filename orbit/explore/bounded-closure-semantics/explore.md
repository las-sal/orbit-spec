# Exploration: bounded-closure-semantics

## Premise

rc13a's first attempt archived without shipping on one contradiction, found by its external pass
after three internal ones missed it:

> **Every RULE carries a CLOSED ENUMERATION of its domain.** … **BOUNDED** | the domain is open;
> the members are named AND an explicit residual is stated

An author names a partial list plus a residual and satisfies the obligation that exists to stop an
open domain reaching propose. rc13b — the stage whose whole job is reading `Closure:` — gets no
honest basis for treating BOUNDED as closed.

It was not carelessness. BOUNDED was introduced deliberately, to be honest about domains that
genuinely cannot be closed, and that need is real: rc02 shipped a NAMED v1 limit (blindness
enforced, byte-exact same-state not) and shipping it was correct. The error is that the honest
escape hatch was filed as a kind of closure, so the word "closed" stopped carrying its obligation.

**The question, and it is the hinge for rc13a and rc13b both**: can "closed" and "open with a stated
residual" both satisfy one obligation, or does the obligation need two tiers with different
consequences?

This exploration applies rc13a's method BY HAND — the stage did not ship, so nothing enforces it.

## Mechanism

### The reframing

The contradiction dissolves once you ask what the obligation is FOR. It was written as "every rule
carries a CLOSED enumeration", and closure was never the point — **the point is that no domain is
open SILENTLY.** rc02 shipped an open domain on purpose and was right to: blindness enforced,
byte-exact same-state not, stated as a named v1 limit in the requirement itself. What must not
happen is a gap nobody wrote down.

So the obligation is ACCOUNTED, not CLOSED, and it has two satisfying outcomes:

| outcome | forms | what it claims |
|---|---|---|
| **CLOSED** | DERIVED (generated by a structure whose parts are known) · SOURCED (exhaustive over a set an authority defines) | every member is covered, and here is why that is provable |
| **OPEN-DECLARED** | BOUNDED | members are covered, a residual REMAINS, and here is its shape |

BOUNDED stops being a third kind of closure and becomes the other way to discharge the obligation.
`Closure:` is then honestly readable by a downstream reader: DERIVED and SOURCED mean closed,
BOUNDED means open-and-declared. Nothing has to pretend.

### What separates an honest BOUNDED from a blank cheque

A residual must CHARACTERIZE what is outside — its KIND and the METHOD by which a member would be
found — not merely assert incompleteness. "There may be others" discharges nothing; it is the
sentence the obligation exists to forbid, wearing the obligation's own vocabulary.

### The consequence difference is VISIBILITY, never BLOCKING

An OPEN-DECLARED domain does not block readiness — if it did, rc02's same-state limit could never
have shipped, and the program would force authors to fake closure to proceed, which is worse than
the contradiction being fixed. Instead the residual is SURFACED and TRAVELS: the readout names it,
and it lands in the change's artifacts (a design risk, a named limit in the requirement) rather than
dying in the exploration. That is the same shape rc02 chose for `budgetExhausted` — reported BESIDE
the state, never as a block, with the decision and its record left to the human.

### Case 1 — run the rule against every bounded claim on the record (2026-09-15)

**Input**: every place this program has declared a domain open, plus one synthetic blank cheque.

**Procedure**: apply the characterization test — does the residual name a KIND and a METHOD?

| bounded claim | kind named? | method named? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| rc02's same-state v1 limit — "blindness is enforced, byte-exact SAME-STATE is not; two passes with no intervening walk may still straddle an unrelated edit" | YES — same-state verification | YES — a per-pass tree fingerprint, assigned to rc04 | **honest OPEN-DECLARED**; shipping it was correct |
| rc13a's move-set residual — "classification will find more; these were found by running two records; the set grows as records accumulate" | YES — a further way a mechanism is wrong | YES — classifying another record | **honest**, marginally; the kind is stated only by negation ("one the nine do not name") |
| rc13a's closure-taxonomy residual — "a justification that fits none of these is a finding about the taxonomy" | YES — another justification form | NO — nothing says how one would be found | **FAILS** — and this is the one that should have been caught internally |
| synthetic: "Domain: states X, Y, Z. Closure: BOUNDED — there may be others." | no | no | **FAILS** — the blank cheque the test exists to reject |

**Finding — the test discriminates on real material, and it fails one of rc13a's own two residuals.**
The move-set residual passes because it names its method; the taxonomy residual does not, and it sat
unchallenged through three passes. That is evidence the test is doing work rather than restating
what an author already knew: applied to the artifact that invented BOUNDED, it rejects half of its
own use.

## Decisions

- **2026-09-15 — D-bcs-1: the obligation is ACCOUNTED, not CLOSED** (ratified in chat by Sal,
  2026-09-15). Closure was never the point — **no domain may be open SILENTLY**. Two outcomes
  discharge the obligation: **CLOSED** (DERIVED or SOURCED — every member covered, with a
  justification saying why that is provable) and **OPEN-DECLARED** (BOUNDED — members covered, a
  residual remains, and the residual NAMES ITS KIND AND METHOD). BOUNDED stops being a third kind of
  closure and becomes the other way to satisfy the obligation, so `Closure:` reads honestly
  downstream and nothing has to pretend.

- **2026-09-15 — D-bcs-2: a residual names its KIND and its METHOD, or it discharges nothing**
  (settled under R-crc-3). "There may be others" is the sentence the obligation exists to forbid,
  wearing the obligation's own vocabulary. Case 1 shows the test does real work: applied to the
  artifact that invented BOUNDED, it passes rc02's same-state limit and the move-set residual and
  REJECTS the closure-taxonomy residual, which named a kind and no method and survived three
  internal passes.

- **2026-09-15 — D-bcs-3: the tier difference is VISIBILITY, never BLOCKING** (settled under
  R-crc-3). An open-declared domain does not block readiness. If it did, rc02's same-state v1 limit
  could never have shipped, and authors would fake closure to proceed — worse than the contradiction
  being fixed. The residual is SURFACED and TRAVELS into the change's artifacts rather than dying in
  the exploration. That is rc02's `budgetExhausted` shape: reported beside the state, the decision
  and its record left to the human.

- **2026-09-15 — D-bcs-4: this is a RECORD, not a check, and that is why it is buildable**
  (settled under R-crc-3, per R-crc-10). Nothing here asks the engine to judge whether an
  enumeration is true or whether a residual is honest. The author DECLARES; the declaration is
  visible; a reader judges. The previous attempt failed precisely because it asked the emit to
  decide closure from prose — R-crc-10 was ratified the same day out of that failure, and this
  decision is its first application.

- **2026-09-15 — D-bcs-5: the recursion is CLOSED OUT deliberately.** The obvious next question —
  "is the kind-and-method test itself a closed enumeration?" — is NOT pursued. The move set went
  five to six to eight to nine by exactly that route, and pursuing it here is how this exploration
  becomes the third stage that ships nothing. The test is a RECORD rule under D-bcs-4; a reader who
  finds a residual that names neither kind nor method has what they need, and a residual form nobody
  has met yet is not a problem this exploration has.
